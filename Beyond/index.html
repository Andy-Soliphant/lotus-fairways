— Beyond Asia —

The world doesn't end at Southeast Asia.

For those who want to extend their journey — or combine Asia with somewhere further afield — we work with trusted partners across Scotland, Africa and the Indian Ocean.

Lotus & Fairways was built on Southeast Asia — but our clients' passions aren't limited to one region, and neither are ours. Scotland has always been part of our story: the golf, the whisky, the landscapes that reward the same spirit of curiosity that drew us to Asia in the first place. South Africa and the Indian Ocean islands complete a picture of luxury travel that spans the world's great destinations without trying to be all things to all people.

Everything we do beyond Asia follows the same principle as everything we do within it: personal knowledge, genuine relationships, and honest advice about what's worth your time.
