Asia by Rail

The great train journeys of Southeast Asia, and the shorter scenic lines we build into a longer journey.

For most of the last century the railways of Southeast Asia were how people moved, and how a few travellers chose to. The romance never quite went away. What has changed is that there are, once again, trains worth taking for their own sake.

We do not sell rail tickets. We build journeys, and a train can be the centre of one or a single beautiful day within it. These are the ones we would choose.

Eastern & Oriental Express

Belmond’s train, back on the rails since 2024, runs three-night journeys out of Singapore through Peninsular Malaysia, with stops such as Penang, Langkawi and the rainforest of Taman Negara, and an occasional longer journey.
