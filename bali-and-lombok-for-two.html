This is a journey for two people who want time together more than a list of sights. It starts in the valleys north of Ubud, climbs into the cool mountains of northern Bali, where the lakes and waterfalls are still quiet, and finishes with six nights by the sea on Lombok, the island across the strait.

The days out are few and chosen: a picnic by a waterfall, a canoe across a crater lake, a morning in the water off the Gili islands. The rest of the time is yours.

The Journey

1Ubud

The Valley — Four Nights

From the airport into the hills to Padma Resort Ubud, set above the Puhu valley north of Ubud, where the infinity pool runs along the edge of the forest. Four nights to settle in. The rice terraces, temples, a Balinese spa or a cooking morning can all be added as you go, and nothing is fixed.

UbudPadma Resort
2Munduk

The Mountains — Three Nights

North into the mountains, where the air is cooler and the hills are covered in coffee and clove. Munduk Moding Plantation sits within a working coffee estate, its pool looking out over the valley. A walk through the rice fields and forest to the Banyuatis waterfall, with a private picnic and a bottle of sparkling wine waiting there. The next day, a guided trek through the rainforest to Lake Tamblingan, across it by handmade canoe to an old temple on the shore, and on to Munduk’s waterfalls.

Lake TamblinganBanyuatis WaterfallMunduk Moding
3Lombok

The Sea — Six Nights

Across the strait to Lombok and Tugu Lombok on Sire Beach, a hotel filled with antiques and old Indonesian art, on a long beach facing the Gili islands. One morning snorkelling off the Gilis; otherwise six nights of nothing in particular, which is the point of ending here.
