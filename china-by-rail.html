The Silk Road was never one road. It was a chain of oasis towns strung across deserts and mountains, and the people who travelled it moved from one to the next for weeks at a time. A train follows that shape better than anything with wings. You cross the country at ground level, and you arrive somewhere that looks nothing like where you left.

Until now there has been no train in China you would take for the train itself. The Silk Road Express is the first: thirty-eight suites, two dining cars set with antique British silver on Jingdezhen porcelain, a 270-degree observation carriage, and a cigar room for two. Between the train nights come the places — the Terracotta Army at Xi’an, the salt flats of Chaka at three thousand metres, two caves at Mogao that most visitors are never shown.

Imperial Splendour

Seven days, Xi’an to Dunhuang. Two nights in the city that was the start of the Silk Road, two aboard the train, and a last night beside the dunes. It also runs the other way, Dunhuang to Xi’an.

The Tang Court

Met at the airport and driven to the Sofitel Legend People’s Grand or the Ritz-Carlton. In the evening, a Tang-dynasty banquet with music and dance at the Tang Dynasty Palace, from seats at the front.

The Terracotta Army

An hour out of the city to the pits of the first emperor’s army — thousands of soldiers and horses, no two faces the same. Dinner from the off-menu list at Xi’an Fanzhuang, then the lit streets of the Grand Tang Everbright City if you have the energy.

The City Wall, then West

A morning bicycle ride along the top of the old city wall and the Hanguangmen gate museum. In the afternoon, first class on the high-speed train to Xining, where the plateau begins. Welcome dinner at the Wanda Vista.

Worth Knowing

Altitude

Xining sits at 2,200 metres and Chaka at 3,000. Take the first day gently. We brief you properly before you go.

Book early for Mogao

Tickets for the Mogao Grottoes are issued against passport details at least thirty days ahead. Book inside that and the caves cannot be promised.

Off the grid

There is no Wi-Fi aboard, and across the desert and the plateau very little signal. Think of it as part of the point.

Families

A child of twelve or under sharing a Family Suite with two adults travels free. Family offers apply to the Deluxe Suites only.
