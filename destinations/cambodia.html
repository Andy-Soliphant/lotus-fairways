— Destination —

Cambodia

Angkor Wat, the Mekong and Phokeethra golf. A country that rewards curiosity more than almost anywhere on earth — if you know where to look beyond the obvious.

Cambodia is the destination that surprises people most — and the one they talk about longest after they return.

Most visitors arrive for Angkor Wat and leave having experienced something they didn't expect: a country of extraordinary warmth, remarkable resilience, and a culture that runs far deeper than the temples. Phnom Penh has quietly become one of Southeast Asia's most interesting cities — its food scene, its riverside setting, its complex history told with extraordinary honesty. The coast at Kep and Kampot offers a slower, greener counterpoint. And for golfers, Phokeethra Country Club in Siem Reap sits among the finest courses in the region. Cambodia has been underestimated for long enough. We have been sending guests there for years, and we have never had one come back disappointed.

When to Go

Best time to visit

November — April

Dry season, cooler temperatures, clear skies. The best conditions for Angkor, golf and the coast. November is particularly special — the Bon Om Touk Water Festival often falls here.

Wet season

May — October

Heavy rain but the landscape turns dramatically green. The Tonle Sap floods and the floating villages move. Still very visitable — a different and often more intimate experience.
