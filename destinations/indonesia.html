Indonesia & Bali

Bali first, and then the islands most people never reach: Lombok, Java and Sumba. Thousands of islands, and the handful of places we would send you.

Bali is the island everyone has an opinion on. The trick is knowing which Bali you want.

There is the Bali of Ubud's valleys and temples, the Bali of clifftops and surf on the Bukit, the calm reef-sheltered beaches of Sanur, and the older, quieter east beneath Mount Agung. Choose well and it is still one of the great islands of Asia. Then there is everything beyond it: Lombok across the strait, Borobudur in Central Java, and the remote beaches of Sumba.

Every hotel below is one we know, with our own verdict on its page. We will tell you which suits you, and which does not.

When to Go

Best time to visit

April — October

The dry season, with lower humidity and clear days across Bali and Lombok. July and August are the busiest months, so book early for those.

Wet season

November — March

Short, heavy downpours rather than days of rain, and the landscape at its greenest. Quieter, and still very good, particularly in Ubud.
