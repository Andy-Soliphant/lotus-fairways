When to Go

The coast & islands

November — April

Dry, calm seas and perfect beach weather in the south. Genuinely prime season — and the reason March and April, difficult in the north, are excellent here. Peak period; book early.

Bangkok & the north

November — early February

The cool season. Comfortable days, clear skies, mountain views around Chiang Mai. This — not the standard "November to April" — is the north's real window. See our note below.

Green season

May — October

Rain in the south and on the islands. But the north comes back into its own — the rains clear the air, everything is green, and we build itineraries that work beautifully around the showers.

Our honest advice — northern Thailand, mid-February to April

Most brochures say Thailand's season is November to April. For the coast, that's right. For Chiang Mai and the north, it isn't. Every year from roughly mid-February, agricultural burning across the region fills the valleys with smoke, and Chiang Mai — sitting in a mountain basin — has nowhere for it to go. In March 2026 the city was ranked the most polluted in the world. We won't send you north in March. We'll send you to the coast, where the weather at that time of year is superb, and we'll get you to Chiang Mai in the window it deserves.
