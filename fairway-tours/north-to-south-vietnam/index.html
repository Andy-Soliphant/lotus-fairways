Why this route

Vietnam in one piece, north to south, the way the country is best understood. The old capital, the karsts of Ha Long, the central coast where the country’s best golf is concentrated, and the energy of Saigon at the end.

Two nights at Ha Long are spent at the golf course itself, so the bay is a morning on the water rather than a day on the road. From there the route only goes south.

The golf builds with the journey: Van Tri, the north’s finest, then Hoiana Shores on the central coast, and a last round at The Bluffs on the dunes east of Saigon.

When to come

The north is cool and grey from January to March, the central coast wet from October to December, and the south hot and humid from May. March to May is when the whole length of the country lines up, and that is when we would build it.

The Journey

A suggested shape, not a fixed package — every course and every night flexes to your group, your handicaps and your pace.

Leg 1

Hanoi3 nights

Day1

Arrive in Hanoi

Private transfer from Noi Bai to your hotel. The evening in the old quarter — the best street food in the country is a short walk in any direction.

Day2

Kings Island Golf Resort Round 1

Out towards Ba Vi mountain, on the lake — the established golf resort in the north and a gentle start.

Day3

Van Tri Golf Club Round 2

A private club north of the city and, by common consent, the finest course in northern Vietnam. Quiet, immaculate, and rarely seen by visitors.

Leg 2

Ha Long Bay2 nights

Day4

Transfer to Ha Long Transfer

East to the coast by private car, around two and a half hours. Checked in at the golf course’s own hotel above the bay.

Day5

FLC Ha Long Bay Golf Club Round 3

Laid across the hills above the bay, with the limestone karsts on the horizon from almost every tee.

Ha Long Bay
Leg 3

Da Nang & Hoi An5 nights

Day6

The bay, then fly south Flight

A private boat among the karsts in the morning, then a flight south to Da Nang.

Day7

Legend Da Nang — Norman Course Round 4

Greg Norman’s dunes course on the coast road — one half of a 36-hole complex, with Jack Nicklaus’s course through the wetlands alongside it.

Day8

At leisure

Hoi An’s old town, the tailors, the beaches, or a cooking class by the river.

Day9

Ba Na Hills Golf Club Round 5

A Luke Donald design in the hills twenty-five minutes inland — dramatic, beautifully conditioned, and a complete change of character from the coast.

Ba Na Hills
Day10

Hoiana Shores Golf Club Round 6

Robert Trent Jones Jr’s true links, six holes on the beach, and the only Vietnamese course in Golf World’s world top 100. The pins carry lanterns rather than flags.

Hoiana Shores
Leg 4

Saigon4 nights

Day11

Fly to Saigon Flight

South to Ho Chi Minh City. Evening at leisure — rooftop bars, street food, or a quiet dinner, all within a block.

Day12

Twin Doves Golf Club Round 7

A private club north of the city, immaculately kept and quiet.

Day13

At leisure

The Reunification Palace, Ben Thanh market, or the Mekong Delta as a day trip.

Day14

The Bluffs Ho Tram Round 8

Greg Norman’s links laid over enormous natural dunes above the sea, and consistently rated among the finest courses in Asia. An early start and two and a half hours to the coast, and worth every minute — the finish.

Day15

Departure

Private transfer to Tan Son Nhat for your flight home.
