Why this route

The whole of Indochina at its own pace. Three weeks, five centres and ten rounds — enough time in each country to see it properly, and never more than one short flight between stops.

It starts gently on the coast at Hua Hin, where Thai golf began, then moves up to Bangkok for the private clubs. From there it crosses into Cambodia for Vattanac, a Faldo course few people have played, and on to Angkor. It finishes with nine nights on Vietnam’s central coast, where the second region is Lang Co over the Hai Van Pass.

The route runs one way from start to finish, and the golf builds with it — the last round is Hoiana Shores, the only Vietnamese course in the world top 100.

When to come

Three countries, three calendars. Central Vietnam’s rains run from October to December, and Thailand and Cambodia turn hot from April. February to early April is when all of them are dry at once, and that is when we would build this journey.

Arrive in Bangkok, on to Hua Hin Transfer

Met on arrival and driven straight to the coast — around three hours, to wake up by the sea rather than in the city.

Pineapple Valley Golf Club Round 1

Formerly Banyan — the name changed, the quality didn’t. Best New Course in Asia when it opened in 2009, carved through the hills behind town.

Pineapple Valley

At leisure

The beach, the night market, the old wooden railway station, or a long lunch by the sea at Khao Takiab.

Black Mountain Golf Club Round 2

Tour-conditioned fairways and a venue for the Asian Tour and the Royal Trophy — the Hua Hin course we return to, and the round the week builds towards.

Black Mountain
Leg 2

Bangkok3 nights

Day5

Transfer to Bangkok Transfer

Back up to the capital by private car. The evening on the river.

Day6

Nikanti Golf Club Round 3

A modern design with a genuinely all-inclusive clubhouse experience — one of the most enjoyable rounds near the city.

Day7

Thai Country Club Round 4

Bangkok’s most prestigious private club — immaculate, exclusive, and the best of the city’s golf. Closed on Mondays, so we route around it.

Thai Country Club
Leg 3

Phnom Penh2 nights

Day8

Fly to Phnom Penh Flight

A short flight into Cambodia. The Royal Palace and the riverfront in the afternoon.

Day9

Vattanac Golf Resort — West Course Round 5

Sir Nick Faldo’s 36-hole design and the reason to come. The West Course was named Cambodia’s best at the 2022 and 2023 World Golf Awards — immaculate, and almost empty.

Vattanac
Leg 4

Siem Reap3 nights

Day10

Fly to Siem Reap Flight

A short hop north. Private transfer to your hotel and the old market quarter for dinner.

Day11

Angkor

Sunrise over Angkor Wat, then Ta Prohm and Angkor Thom before the heat, with a private guide who knows how to tell it.

Day12

Angkor Golf Resort Round 6

Nick Faldo’s design and Golf Digest’s pick as the best course in Cambodia. Water borders every hole, and the greens are quick and full of subtle break.

Leg 5

Da Nang & Hoi An9 nights

Day13

Fly to Da Nang Flight

Direct from Siem Reap to Vietnam’s central coast. Private transfer to your hotel.

Day14

Montgomerie Links Round 7

Colin Montgomerie’s links-style layout on land adjoining Legend — firm, breezy and open to the sea wind.

Day15

At leisure

Hoi An’s old town, the tailors, the beaches, or a cooking class by the river.

Day16

Laguna Lang Co Round 8

Sir Nick Faldo’s course between the mountains and the sea at Lang Co, over the Hai Van Pass — the drive there is half the day’s pleasure.

Day17

Hue

The imperial city, two hours north — the citadel, the royal tombs and the Perfume River.

Day18

At leisure

The Marble Mountains, the beach, or the spa.

Day19

Ba Na Hills Golf Club Round 9

A Luke Donald design in the hills twenty-five minutes inland — dramatic, beautifully conditioned, and a complete change of character from the coast.

Ba Na Hills
Day20

At leisure

A slow day before the finish.

Day21

Hoiana Shores Golf Club Round 10

Robert Trent Jones Jr’s true links, six holes on the beach, and the only Vietnamese course in Golf World’s world top 100. The pins carry lanterns rather than flags. The finish.

Hoiana Shores
Day22

Departure

Private transfer to Da Nang International for your flight home.
