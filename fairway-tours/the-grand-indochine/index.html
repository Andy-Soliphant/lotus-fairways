The Journey

Three countries, unhurried — the whole of Indochina at its own pace. Our most complete journey: deeper into each country, more courses, more time to breathe between them.

1

Thailand

7 nights

Bangkok's championship golf, then north to Chiang Mai's mountains or south to Hua Hin's coast — two Thai centres, not one.

2

Cambodia

5 nights

Phnom Penh for Vattanac, a top-class Faldo course few have played, then Siem Reap for Angkor Golf Resort beside the temples. Sunrise at Angkor Wat.

3

Vietnam

9 nights

The central coast — Laguna Lang Co, Ba Na Hills, Hoiana Shores — with time for a second region, finishing on the beach at Hoi An.

Tailoring
