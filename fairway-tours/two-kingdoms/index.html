Why this route

Two golfing nations and only one internal flight. Three nights in Bangkok for the capital’s best, then straight across to the central coast of Vietnam, where the golf is the reason for the trip.

Around Da Nang and Hoi An a group can play courses by Robert Trent Jones Jr, Greg Norman, Jack Nicklaus, Colin Montgomerie and Luke Donald, all inside about forty minutes. Nowhere else in Asia puts that many names so close together.

Both cities connect easily with the UK, so you can fly in to one and home from the other — no doubling back. It finishes on Hoiana Shores, a true links on the beach and the only Vietnamese course in the world top 100.

When to come

Bangkok is good from November, but central Vietnam’s rains run from October to December. February to April is when both ends are at their best.

Nikanti Golf Club Round 1

A modern design with a genuinely all-inclusive clubhouse experience — one of the most enjoyable rounds near the city, and the right way to shake off the flight.

Day3

Thai Country Club Round 2

Bangkok’s most prestigious private club — immaculate, exclusive, and the best of the city’s golf. The club is closed on Mondays, so we route around it.

Thai Country Club
Leg 2

Da Nang & Hoi An7 nights

Day4

Fly to Da Nang Flight

A short direct flight east to Vietnam’s central coast. Private transfer to your hotel, and Hoi An’s old town after dark.

Day5

Montgomerie Links Round 3

Colin Montgomerie’s links-style layout on land adjoining Legend — firm, breezy and open to the sea wind.

Day6

At leisure

Hoi An’s old town, the tailors, the beaches, or a cooking class by the river.

Day7

Legend Da Nang — Norman Course Round 4

Greg Norman’s dunes course on the coast road — one half of a 36-hole complex, with Jack Nicklaus’s course through the wetlands alongside it.

Day8

Ba Na Hills Golf Club Round 5

A Luke Donald design in the hills twenty-five minutes inland — dramatic, beautifully conditioned, and a complete change of character from the coast.

Ba Na Hills
Day9

At leisure

The Marble Mountains, the Hai Van Pass by car, or simply the pool.

Day10

Hoiana Shores Golf Club Round 6

Robert Trent Jones Jr’s true links, six holes on the beach, and the only Vietnamese course in Golf World’s world top 100. The pins carry lanterns rather than flags. The finish.

Hoiana Shores
Day11

Departure

Private transfer to Da Nang International for your flight home.
