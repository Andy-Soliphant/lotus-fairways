Siem Reap & Angkor

Phokeethra Country Club is, in our view, the most underrated championship course in Southeast Asia — Nick Faldo's design in immaculate condition, with the particular quality of conditioning and service that comes from a resort course that takes itself seriously. Playing a world-class round in the morning and visiting Angkor Wat at sunset in the same day is something no other destination on earth can offer. Siem Reap does it effortlessly, and at a price point that makes the experience exceptional value.

After the round

Angkor Wat at sunrise or sunset is an experience that transcends the itinerary. Beyond the main temple, the jungle-covered ruins of Ta Prohm and the Bayon's carved faces reward those who explore. Siem Reap town has a good restaurant scene — the Old Market area offers the best dining, with the Pub Street area well-suited to golf groups winding down after a long day.

Phnom Penh

Phnom Penh has quietly become one of Southeast Asia's most interesting cities — its food scene, its riverfront setting and its complex history told with remarkable honesty. The golf here is genuinely strong: Vattanac Golf Resort is top class, one of the finest layouts in Cambodia, and a course that stands on its own merits rather than relying on the destination's cultural backdrop. Combine with Siem Reap for a complete Cambodia golf break, or use Phnom Penh as a compelling standalone destination for groups who want serious golf alongside real cultural depth.

After the round

The Phnom Penh riverfront at night is one of the more atmospheric settings in Southeast Asia — the confluence of the Mekong and Tonle Sap rivers, restaurants with views across the water, a bar scene that has grown considerably without losing its character. The Foreign Correspondents' Club terrace remains one of the great sunset spots in Asia. The city's Khmer cuisine has developed substantially — excellent options alongside strong international dining.

When to Go

Best time to visit

November — April

Dry season — cool mornings, clear skies, ideal playing conditions. November is particularly atmospheric — the Water Festival often falls here.

Wet season

May — October

Heavy rain but the landscape turns dramatically green. Still visitable — a more intimate experience with fewer visitors at the temples.

How to Combine Destinations

Cambodia works well as a 7-10 night destination in its own right, or as part of a broader Southeast Asia itinerary combined with Thailand or Vietnam.

7 nights

Siem Reap Focus

5 nights Siem Reap — Phokeethra (2 rounds), Angkor temples, optional day trip to Tonle Sap. The ideal introduction to Cambodia golf.

10 nights

Full Cambodia

5 nights Siem Reap (Phokeethra, Angkor), fly to Phnom Penh for 4 nights (Vattanac, Chhun On, Mekong riverfront). The complete Cambodia experience.

16–18 nights

Vietnam + Cambodia

Da Nang for Hoiana Shores and the central coast, Ho Chi Minh City for The Bluffs, then Siem Reap to finish. Vietnam's golf depth combined with Cambodia's extraordinary setting.
