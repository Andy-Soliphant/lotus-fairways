The Trip

Private-club golf by day, one of the world's great cities by night — Bangkok does both, and does them well.

Bangkok is chronically underrated as a golf destination — and those who discover it rarely overlook it again. Over sixty championship layouts sit within an hour of the centre, maintained to a standard that satisfies the most discerning player.

Three rounds across Thai Country Club, Nikanti and Alpine, with the city's extraordinary food and river scene filling the evenings. Green fees, caddies and private transfers all handled; the hotel is your choice.

The natural first leg of a longer trip — ask us about adding Hua Hin, Chiang Mai, or Da Nang across the border in Vietnam.

Why Bangkok

Asia's great golf capital. More than fifty courses lie within reach of the city — we've played most, and show you the ones we return to. Championship layouts by day; one of the world's most exciting cities by night.

Three rounds in a week is the right rhythm here — and we route them deliberately, opening easy and building to Alpine, so the best golf meets your best swing.
