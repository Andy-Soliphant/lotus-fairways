The Trip

Private-club golf by day, one of the world's great cities by night — Bangkok does both, and does them well.

Bangkok is chronically underrated as a golf destination — and those who discover it rarely overlook it again. Over sixty championship layouts sit within an hour of the centre, maintained to a standard that satisfies the most discerning player.

Three rounds across Thai Country Club, Nikanti and Alpine, with the city's extraordinary food and river scene filling the evenings. Green fees, caddies and private transfers all handled; the hotel is your choice.

The natural first leg of a longer Thailand trip — ask us about adding Hua Hin or Phuket.

The Week

A suggested shape — every day flexes to your group, your handicaps and your pace. Nothing here is fixed in stone.

Day1

Arrival

Private transfer from Suvarnabhumi to your riverside or city hotel. Evening at leisure in one of the world's great food cities.

Day2

Thai Country Club Round 1

Bangkok's most prestigious private club — immaculate, exclusive, and a fitting opening round.

Day3

At leisure

The Grand Palace, the river, the markets, or a rooftop dinner above the Chao Phraya. Bangkok rewards a day off the course.

Day4

Nikanti Golf Club Round 2

A modern design with a genuinely all-inclusive clubhouse experience — one of the most enjoyable rounds near the city.

Day5

At leisure

Time for the spa, the tailors, Chinatown, or the river temples — entirely as you like it.

Day6

Alpine Golf & Sports Club Round 3

A championship layout that has hosted the Thailand Open — a strong, satisfying finish to the week's golf.

Day7

At leisure

A last day in the city before you leave — or the start of your onward leg to the coast.

Day8

Departure

Private transfer to the airport in good time for your flight.
