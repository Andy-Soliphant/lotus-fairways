Phuket

Phuket is where most international golfers begin their Thailand journey — and for good reason. The island combines championship courses with genuinely world-class hotels, the Andaman Sea, and an evening culture that golf groups return for repeatedly. It is the most complete golf destination in Thailand, and the one we recommend most often for first-time visitors.

After the round

Patong and Bangla Road for those who want it. Surin and Kata for those who don't. Phuket accommodates both without forcing either. Sunset beach bars, private villa dinners, rooftop restaurants — the evening options are genuinely exceptional and suit all tastes within a group.

Bangkok

Bangkok is chronically underrated as a golf destination — and those who discover it rarely overlook it again. Over sixty championship layouts sit within an hour of the city centre, courses that have hosted Asian Tour events and are maintained to a standard that would satisfy the most discerning player. Add one of the world's great cities as your evening backdrop and Bangkok becomes not just a stopover but a destination in its own right.

After the round

Bangkok's food scene is one of the world's best — from street-level Chinatown to rooftop restaurants above the Chao Phraya. The city's nightlife is legendary. Temples, markets and river bars for those who want something quieter. Bangkok does everything and does it well.

  • Mandarin Oriental — a Bangkok institution, river setting
  • Park Hyatt Bangkok — refined, central, exceptional service
  • Rosewood Bangkok — design-led, strong F&B programme
  • The Peninsula — classic luxury, superb river views

Hua Hin

If the priority is the golf above everything else, Hua Hin is where you go. Black Mountain consistently ranks among the top ten courses in Asia — tour conditioning, extraordinary caddies and a layout that rewards every level of player. The royal town itself is relaxed and charming, and its natural pairing with Bangkok (two and a half hours by road) makes it ideal for the second half of a Bangkok itinerary or as a standalone golf destination.

After the round

Hua Hin's night market is one of Thailand's best. The beach town has a good selection of restaurants and bars without the intensity of Phuket or Pattaya — ideal for groups who want a relaxed evening after a serious day on the course. Bangkok is an easy road transfer for those wanting more.

Our Hotels

  • Centara Grand Beach Resort — established, well-positioned

Pattaya

No destination in Asia has the depth of Pattaya's course lineup. Siam Country Club, Laem Chabang, Chee Chan — courses that have hosted major Asian Tour events and offer some of the most challenging and rewarding golf in the region. Two hours from Bangkok, it pairs naturally with the capital for longer itineraries. Stay at the right hotel and Pattaya delivers a golf experience that rivals anywhere on the continent.

When to Go

Thailand's geography means weather varies by region. Booking the wrong destination at the wrong time of year is the most avoidable mistake in Thailand golf planning — we always advise on this before confirming an itinerary.

Prime season

November — April

Dry season across most of Thailand. Cool, clear mornings. Calm seas in the south. Peak golf season — book courses and hotels early for December and January.

Shoulder season

May & October

Quieter and better value. Bangkok and Chiang Mai remain excellent. The south can be unpredictable — we advise on the best islands and coastal destinations for this window.

Wet season

June — September

Heavy rain in the south and on the islands. Bangkok and the north remain viable — we build itineraries that work in the wet season and are honest about what works and what doesn't.

How to Combine Destinations

Most golfers spend 10–14 nights in Thailand, moving between two or three destinations. Here are the combinations we recommend most often.

10 nights

Phuket + Hua Hin

5 nights Phuket (Laguna, Red Mountain, Blue Canyon), fly or drive to Hua Hin, 4 nights (Black Mountain, Banyan). The island experience combined with Thailand's finest pure golf course.

12 nights

Bangkok + Hua Hin + Phuket

3 nights Bangkok (Thai Country Club, Nikanti), road transfer to Hua Hin, 3 nights, fly to Phuket for the final 5 nights. The complete Thailand golf journey.

14 nights

Bangkok + Pattaya + Phuket

Bangkok for the private clubs, Pattaya for the deepest course lineup in Asia (Siam CC, Laem Chabang, Chee Chan), then Phuket for the island finish. Maximum golf variety.
