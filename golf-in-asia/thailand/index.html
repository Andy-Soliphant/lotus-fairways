Bangkok

Asia's great golf capital. More than fifty courses lie within reach of the city — we've played most, and show you the ones we return to. Championship layouts by day; one of the world's most exciting cities by night.

Alpine Golf & Sports Club

Ronald M. Garl · 18 holes · par 72

“Alpine is always on our must-play list — if you're in Bangkok, we suggest you add it to yours.”

Host of the 2000 & 2004 Johnnie Walker Classic — Tiger Woods won in 2000, eagling the par-5 7th. Widely rated among Thailand's finest.

Nikanti Golf Club

Pirapon Namatra · 18 holes · par 72

“Six-hole circuits and an all-inclusive package — a stunning course in every way.”

Nominated Best Course in Thailand every year since 2020. 'Nikanti' means 'Attachment to the State of Delightfulness' in Pali.

Thai Country Club

Denis Griffiths · 18 holes · par 72

“Good enough for Tiger, good enough for me and you.”

Host of the 1997 Asian Honda Classic — Tiger Woods won by 10 strokes and famously drove the par-4 10th green. Later hosted the Volvo Masters of Asia and Thailand Open.

The RG City Golf Club (formerly Royal Gems Golf City)

Ron Garl · 18 holes · par 72

“A selection of iconic holes and the back nine of Augusta — always worth a visit.”

Asia's first and only replica course — a front nine of the world's most famous holes and a back nine mirroring Augusta National.
Also in Bangkok
Amata Spring Country Club
Lee Schmidt (Schmidt-Curley Design)
One of Thailand's most exclusive clubs, host of the Thailand Championship.
Bangkok Golf Club
Chird Boonyarattanevet · 18 holes
Opened 1993 in Pathum Thani, about 35 minutes north of the city centre.
Lam Luk Ka Country Club
Roger Packard (1994)
The West course brings water into play on fifteen holes — no pushover.
Legacy Golf Club
Jack Nicklaus
The only Nicklaus course in the Bangkok area, opened 1989 (formerly Natural Park Ramindra).
Muang Kaew Golf Club
Schmidt-Curley (2003 redesign)
Seni Thirawat's 1992 original, redesigned by Schmidt-Curley — convenient to the city.
Riverdale Golf Club
Morrow-Tikkanen Design
A modern favourite on the Chao Phraya, by Jonathan Morrow & Al Tikkanen.
Subhapruek Golf Club
Pete Dye · 18 holes · par 72
The only Pete Dye design in Thailand — palm-lined fairways, water on nearly every hole, and a par-4 18th modelled on Sawgrass.
Summit Windmill Golf Club
Nick Faldo · 18 holes
Close to Suvarnabhumi airport.
Thana City Golf Club
Greg Norman · 18 holes
A parkland test on the city's eastern edge.

Hua Hin

The royal resort town, and Thailand's most relaxed golf. A dozen courses along this stretch of coast — from Black Mountain's tour-conditioned fairways to the hills behind town. Where the golf comes first and the pace stays gentle.

Black Mountain Golf Club

Phil Ryan (Pacific Coast Design) · 27 holes · par 72

“A Hua Hin go-to course, and one of mine too.”

Host of the True Thailand Classic (co-sanctioned Asian & European Tour) and the Black Mountain Masters; regularly ranked among Thailand's very best.

Pineapple Valley Golf Club (formerly Banyan Golf Club)

Pirapon Namatra · 18 holes · par 72

“Pineapple, formerly known as Banyan. The name changed, the quality didn't.”

Voted Thailand's Best Golf Club 2023 by 15,000 golfers; Rolex Top-1,000 in the world; Golf Digest Thailand's 6th best (2022).
Also in Hua Hin
Imperial Lake View Resort & Golf Club
Roger Packard · 36 holes
A 36-hole facility of four distinct nines, opened 1993, with later input from Ronald Fream.
Majestic Creek Country Club
Dr Sukitti Klangvisai · 27 holes · par 72
A 27-hole 'sleeping giant' in the foothills 25 minutes west of town — meandering creeks, mountain backdrop toward the Burma border, three distinct nines.
Palm Hills Golf Resort
Max Wexler
Bermuda fairways and greens, a short drive from town.
Sea Pines Golf Course
Maj Gen Weerayudth Phetbuasak
Opened 2010, owned and run by the Royal Thai Army, near the town.
Springfield Royal Country Club
Jack Nicklaus · 27 holes
A 27-hole Nicklaus design.
Royal Hua Hin Golf Club
18 holes
Thailand's first standard course, laid out in 1924 beside the railway.

Pattaya

The east coast's golf powerhouse. Twenty-odd courses within an hour, more than anywhere else in Thailand — including Siam Country Club, where the LPGA plays. Big golf, and the great reclining Buddha watching over Chee Chan.

Chee Chan Golf Resort

David Dale (Golfplan) · 18 holes · par 72

“Set under the gaze of a magnificent Buddha, you'll be sure to enjoy this setting. We can't guarantee Buddha will improve your game though.”

Rated among the most beautiful modern courses in Asia; dramatic setting beneath the Khao Chee Chan Buddha carving.

Laem Chabang International

Jack Nicklaus · 27 holes · par 72

“With three nine holes covering mountains, lakes and valleys, be sure to pick the 18 that work for you.”

A Nicklaus Signature course over 700 acres; regularly nominated Best Course in Thailand at the World Golf Awards.

Siam Country Club

Schmidt-Curley (renovation)

“World-class golf, whatever the town's reputation — the Old Course is a genuine top-100.”

Home of the Honda LPGA Thailand; the Old Course ranks among the world's top 100.
Also in Pattaya
Bangpra International Golf Club
Japan Golf Promotion (1958)
A lakeside classic dating to 1958, later updated by JMP Golf Design.
Burapha Golf Club
Gary Panks & David Graham · 36 holes
A 36-hole layout by Gary Panks with David Graham.
Khao Kheow Country Club
Pete Dye, Perry Dye & Cynthia Dye McGarey (Dye Designs) · 27 holes
A 27-hole Pete Dye design in the hills behind the coast.
Pattana Golf Resort
Gassan Co. · 27 holes
Opened 2004, three distinct nines across a 400-acre resort.
Phoenix Gold Golf & Country Club
Denis Griffiths (1993) · 27 holes
27 holes — Ocean, Lake and Mountain — with the Lakes nine later reworked by Pirapon Namatra.
St Andrews 2000
Desmond Muirhead · 18 holes · par 72
Themed on the Old Course at St Andrews — Desmond Muirhead recreated the look and feel of the home of golf in the Pattaya hills.

Phuket

Island golf at its finest. A handful of genuinely world-class courses across the island — Blue Canyon's championship pedigree, Red Mountain's drama, Aquella's coastal calm — then the Andaman beaches to finish.

Aquella Golf & Country Club

Pacific Coast Design (Paul Reeves & Phil Ryan) · 18 holes · par 72

“A stunning sea-view course, and one of our team's go-to rounds in Asia.”

Built on the former Thai Muang site along the Andaman coast; timber-faced bunkers echo the original Pete Dye layout.

Blue Canyon Country Club

Yoshikazu Kato

“Home to the Tiger Hole — a challenge worthy of anyone.”

Host of the Johnnie Walker Classic 1994/1998/2007 — Tiger Woods won here in 1998, coming from eight shots back with a final-round 65.

Red Mountain Golf Club

Jon Morrow & Al Tikkanen · 18 holes · par 72

“Set in a stunning landscape, and a real test of golf. We love a challenge, and we love stunning — so Red Mountain is a natural fit.”

Golf Digest 'Best in Thailand' 2014; widely rated the finest course on Phuket.
Also in Phuket
Blue Canyon Lakes Course
Yoshikazu Kato · 18 holes
The Lakes course, opened February 1999 — sister to the championship Canyon Course.
Laguna Golf Phuket
Paul Jansen (2014 redesign) · 18 holes · par 71
A resort course reborn by Paul Jansen — shaped greens, lagoons and coconut groves. Signature 17th is a 612-yard par 5 over water twice.
Loch Palm Golf Club
Dr Sukitti Klangvisai (1993)
Set around a lake, opened 1993, with upgrades by Jon Morrow across 2013-19.
Mission Hills Phuket Golf Club
Jack Nicklaus · 18 holes · par 72
A Nicklaus design opened 2004, framed by mangroves with Andaman Sea views.
Phuket Country Club
Dr Sukitti Klangvisai · 18 holes · par 72
The first golf course built on Phuket. Its famous par-5 10th wraps 180° around a lake — reachable in one if you dare carry 240 yards of water.

Chiang Mai

The cool north. Mountain courses in the foothills, a gentler climate, and the cultural heart of old Lanna between rounds — a change of pace from the coasts and the capital.

Alpine Golf Resort Chiang Mai

Ronald M. Garl · 18 holes · par 72

“Set in the foothills of the mountains, you'll find a stunning course. It's worth the journey.”

Sister course to Alpine Bangkok; host of the 2013 & 2014 Asian Tour Chiang Mai Classic. Widely rated among the finest in the north.

Chiang Mai Highlands

Schmidt-Curley (Lee Schmidt) · 18 holes · par 72

“Set amongst the stunning mountains — a course we love to play.”

Ranked Thailand's #14 course; built on a 'power spot' — the former hermitage of the revered monk Kuba Sivichai.
Also in Chiang Mai
Chiangmai Inthanon Golf Resort
Supachai Silamom
A mountain course in a natural resort setting (North Hill).

Pravit Raeng-Po

Gassan Legacy
Schmidt-Curley (2014 renovation) · 18 holes · par 72
Rebuilt by Schmidt-Curley — the firm behind Chiang Mai Highlands — with expansive water and a championship layout.
Mae Jo Golf Club
Seni Thirawat (2004) · 18 holes · par 72
Zoysia fairways and Korean Velvet greens, about 20 minutes from downtown Chiang Mai.
Royal Chiang Mai
Peter Thomson · 18 holes · par 72
Designed by five-time Open champion Peter Thomson — a British parkland style with links touches, heavily tree-lined with water on nearly every hole.
Summit Green Valley
Dennis Griffiths · 18 holes · par 72
Same designer as Thai Country Club — a Lanna-style championship course with water on nearly every hole and a testing par-4 9th to an island green.
