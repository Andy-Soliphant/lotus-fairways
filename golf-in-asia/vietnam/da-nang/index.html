The Trip

Links golf on the ocean, a UNESCO old town, and the best value in Asian golf right now.

Vietnam's central coast has quietly become the most compelling golf destination in Southeast Asia. Hoiana Shores is a genuine links — natural dunes, sea wind, firm ground — and it sits half an hour from Hoi An, a UNESCO old town that no beach resort in Thailand can answer.

Three rounds across Hoiana Shores, Ba Na Hills and Legend Da Nang. Between them: Hoi An's tailors and lanterns, the Hai Van Pass, and beaches that run for miles. Green fees, caddies and private transfers all handled; the hotel is your choice.

The caddies here are, in our experience, the best in Asia — and that is not a small thing over four hours.

The Week

A suggested shape — every day flexes to your group, your handicaps and your pace. Nothing here is fixed in stone.

Day1

Arrival

Private transfer from Da Nang International to your hotel on the beach or in Hoi An. Evening at leisure — the old town is at its best after dark.

Day2

Hoiana Shores Golf Club Round 1

Robert Trent Jones II's links on the South China Sea. Natural dunes, ocean views throughout, and genuine links character — one of Asia's great courses by any measure.

Day3

At leisure

Hoi An's old town, the tailors, the beaches, or a cooking class by the river. Da Nang's Marble Mountains are twenty minutes away.

Day4

Ba Na Hills Golf Club Round 2

A Luke Donald design in the hills above Da Nang — dramatic, beautifully conditioned, and a complete change of character from the coast.

Day5

At leisure

The Hai Van Pass by car, a day on the sand, or simply the pool. Hue is a two-hour drive north for those who want the imperial city.

Day6

Legend Da Nang — Norman Course Round 3

Greg Norman's original dunes course on the coast road — now one half of a 36-hole complex, with Jack Nicklaus's course alongside it. Firm, breezy, and a strong finish to the week's golf.

Day7

At leisure

A last day on the central coast — beach, spa, or a farewell dinner in the old town.

Day8

Departure

Private transfer to Da Nang International in good time for your flight.
