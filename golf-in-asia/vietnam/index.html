Central Vietnam

Vietnam's golf coast, and the heart of any trip here. A run of world-class courses along one stretch of shore — Hoiana Shores, for many the finest in Asia, then Ba Na Hills in the clouds and Laguna Lang Co by the sea — with the lantern-lit beauty of Hoi An to finish each day.

Ba Na Hills Golf Club

Luke Donald · 18 holes · par 72

“Magnificent views, and a joy to play.”

Luke Donald's first-ever design; voted Vietnam's Best Golf Course several years running. All 18 holes floodlit for night play.

Hoiana Shores Golf Club

Robert Trent Jones Jr · 18 holes · par 71

“This may be my favourite course in Asia. I'd play it every day.”

Robert Trent Jones Jr's first course in Vietnam; host of the Women's Amateur Asia-Pacific.

Laguna Golf Lang Co

Nick Faldo · 18 holes · par 71

“Set in the foothills of the mountains overlooking the sea — the setting is one of Asia's finest.”

Faldo's second Vietnam design, widely rated one of his finest; hosts the Faldo Series Asia finals.
Also in Central Vietnam
Golden Sands Golf Resort
Nicklaus Design · 18 holes · par 71
A Nicklaus Design links on the coast about 20km east of Hue — the newest addition to Central Vietnam's golf corridor.
Legend Da Nang Golf Resort
Greg Norman & Jack Nicklaus · 36 holes · par 72
A rare two-designer resort on the Da Nang shore — play a Norman links and a Nicklaus signature in the same trip, on the same beach.
Montgomerie Links Vietnam
Colin Montgomerie · 18 holes · par 72
Colin Montgomerie's design set between Da Nang beach and the UNESCO town of Hoi An, with the Marble Mountains as a backdrop — rated among Asia's ten most elite courses by Forbes in 2012.
Vinpearl Golf Nam Hoi An
IMG · 18 holes · par 72
A links-style course 17km south of Hoi An, part of a large Vinpearl beach resort — wide fairways, white-sand bunkers, two drivable par-4s, welcoming to all levels.

Ho Chi Minh City

The energy of the south, and a coast that hides a giant. Saigon itself for the buzz, then The Bluffs at Ho Tram — Greg Norman's links among the world's top 100 — an hour down the coast. A great city, and a bucket-list round beside it.

The Bluffs Ho Tram Strip

Greg Norman · 18 holes · par 71

“Sergio loved it. So do we.”

Ranked No. 35 in Golf Digest's World 100 Greatest Courses and Vietnam's No. 1; host of the Ho Tram Open. One of the only true links courses in Vietnam.
Also in Ho Chi Minh City
Vinpearl Golf Léman
~7,700 yds (36 holes) · 36 holes · par 72
A brand-new 36-hole complex (North & South) in Củ Chi, an hour from the city, opened October 2025 — a 'Riparian Habitat' design following the riverine landscape, reinterpreting four classic template holes (Dell, Biarritz, Redan, Volcano). Among the longest courses in Asia.
Long Thanh Golf Club
36 holes · par 72
A 36-hole club on a hill surrounded by the Dong Nai river, east of the city — tree-lined fairways to soft Bermuda TifEagle greens, a long-standing Saigon favourite.
Twin Doves Golf Club
27 holes · par 72
A 27-hole members' course in Binh Duong, ~1.5 hrs from the city — three nines (Luna, Mare, Sole) with paspalum turf, one of the best-conditioned layouts in the Saigon area. The Sole nine features an island green.
Vietnam Golf & Country Club
36 holes · par 72
The nearest 36-hole club to central Ho Chi Minh City, in Thu Duc — mature, tree-lined, with the East Course designed by six-time Major winner Lee Trevino.

Phan Thiet

The quiet coast most golfers miss. A handful of courses along the dunes at Mui Ne, led by NovaWorld — Vietnam's only PGA-licensed course, another Norman design — beside one of our favourite beaches.

PGA NovaWorld Phan Thiet

Greg Norman · 36 holes · par 72

“The only PGA course, and by one of our favourite beaches. It's got to spell winner!”

Vietnam's first and only licensed PGA course; Best New Course in Asia-Pacific at the 2022 Asian Golf Awards.
Also in Phan Thiet
Sea Links Golf & Country Club
Ronald Fream (Golfplan) · 18 holes · par 72
A dramatic links-style layout on an 80-metre clifftop above Mui Ne Bay — huge contoured greens, long par-5s and two island par-3s, billed as one of Asia's most challenging links.

Hanoi

The capital, and the north's proving ground. Van Tri, widely rated the finest course in the north, anchors a growing spread of golf around the city — with the Old Quarter, the lake and the street food between rounds.

Van Tri Golf Club

Peter Rousseau · 18 holes · par 72

“Renowned as the course to play in the north.”

Vietnam's first exclusively private golf club; 'Tiger Tees' stretch past 7,200 yards.
Also in Hanoi
Hanoi Golf Club
Susumu Fujiwara · 18 holes · par 72
A Japanese-managed course in Soc Son, 40 minutes north of the city over 108 hectares of gently sloping terrain — functional, well-run, with a clubhouse designed around Japanese spiritual aesthetics.
Long Bien Golf Course
Nelson & Haworth · 27 holes · par 72
A 27-hole course inside a loop of the Red River, close to the city centre — popular and busy, best played midweek.
Thanh Lanh Valley Golf Club
Nick Faldo · 18 holes · par 72
A Nick Faldo design in a peaceful valley beside Thanh Lanh Lake below the Tam Dao range — the signature par-3 8th plays to a heart-shaped green set dramatically over the water. About 90 minutes from the city.
Kings Island Golf Resort
36 holes
A long-established resort on the Dong Mo lake at the foot of Ba Vi mountain, about an hour west — reached by boat across the lake, with lakeside and mountainside courses.
Phoenix Golf Resort
54 holes · par 72
The biggest golf complex in Vietnam, an hour west of Hanoi — limestone outcrops that echo Halong Bay frame many holes, and the links-style Champion Course stretches to 7,486 yards.

Halong & Haiphong

Golf above the bay. A cluster of courses along this stretch of coast — FLC Ha Long and Tuan Chau right on the water — paired with a night cruising the limestone islands of Halong itself.

FLC Ha Long Bay Golf Club

Schmidt-Curley · 18 holes · par 71

“Set above the bay, it offers outstanding views and an outstanding challenge — one to take on!”

The only golf course in Vietnam with panoramic views over Halong Bay.

Tuan Chau Golf Resort

Pacific Coast Design · 18 holes · par 72

“Immaculate greens alongside the majestic bay — what's not to love?”

Half the course borders Halong Bay itself — built on reclaimed land on Tuan Chau Island.
Also in Halong & Haiphong
BRG Ruby Tree Golf Resort
Pacific Coast Design · 18 holes · par 72
A seaside course at Do Son, ~20km from Hai Phong — front to the sea, backed by the Dragon mountain range; voted Vietnam's Most Prospective Golf Course in 2009.
Dragon Golf Links
Greg Norman · 27 holes · par 72
A Greg Norman links on a semi-private island at Do Son — 27 holes with sea views, opened 2023, 25 minutes from Hai Phong city.
PGA Silk Path Dong Trieu Golf & Country Club
Nick Faldo · 27 holes · par 72
Sir Nick Faldo's newest Vietnam design, routed around granite outcrops and waterways through the low mountains of Dong Trieu — river-crossing holes, bunker-ringed greens and wavy 'potato chip' putting surfaces. An 18-hole championship course plus a 9-hole par-3.
Vinpearl Golf Hai Phong
IMG · 36 holes · par 72
A 36-hole Vinpearl complex on Vu Yen Island — the Lakeside course with expansive water, the Marshland course over rugged wetland terrain.

The Far North

Golf at the edge of the map. High in the mountains near the Chinese border, a single extraordinary course where your tee shot aims towards China — reason enough to make the journey north.

Sapa Grand Golf Course

18 holes · par 71

“Few places offer a tee shot aimed into China, but here you do — amid the splendour of the Vietnamese countryside. A one-off.”
