The Grove, Cromer

Set just moments from Cromer's clifftops, The Grove offers a warm, country-house welcome shaped by centuries of history. The Georgian house dates back some 250 years, and has been a guest house since 1936 — nine decades of hosting family holidays, golf breaks and quiet coastal escapes under family ownership. There's a calm that settles the moment you arrive, the kind that makes The Grove an easy fit for any type of stay, golf included.

Set Within Four Acres

The house sits within four acres of grounds, with a kitchen garden, woodland walks and an indoor heated pool for guests who want to unwind after a round. A short walk through the trees leads to the clifftop path and down to the beach — close enough to Cromer's town, pier and shops for an easy stroll, yet tucked away enough to feel genuinely restful.

Dining at The Garden Rooms

The Garden Rooms restaurant is the heart of the house — light-filled, seasonal, and rooted in Norfolk produce. Menus change with what's grown, landed or produced nearby: Cromer crab at its best, garden vegetables picked hours before service, and local meats cooked simply and well. It's the kind of place where a post-round dinner or a relaxed breakfast both feel considered, without ever feeling fussy.

Sundown

Among the trees on the front lawn sits Sundown, The Grove's tipi restaurant — relaxed by day, atmospheric after dark, with festoon lights and easy conversation running long into the evening. Stone-baked pizzas, Norfolk tapas and laid-back cocktails make it a natural spot to end a day on the coast or the course.

Rooms

Rooms at The Grove mix period character with modern comfort — exposed brick, garden views, and quiet, well-appointed spaces to rest up between rounds.

A Base for Golf in North Norfolk

With Royal Cromer, Sheringham and the wider North Norfolk coast all close by, The Grove makes an ideal base for a golfing break — a relaxed, well-fed stay bookending days on some of England's finest links.
