Most visitors see one Indonesian island. This journey sees five, and each one shows a different country. Java has the temples, Borneo has the rainforest and the orangutans, East Java has the volcanoes, and Bali and Lombok are where it slows down.

It is an active trip and we will be straight about that. There are early starts, three internal flights, and two nights on a traditional river boat in Borneo, which is comfortable rather than luxurious. In return you will see things that very few people combine in one journey, and the last six nights are spent in Ubud and by the sea.

The Journey

1Borobudur

The Temples — Three Nights

Arrive at Yogyakarta and go straight to Prambanan, the great ninth-century Hindu temple, before driving on to Plataran Heritage Borobudur in the hills near the temple. Sunrise over Borobudur the next morning, then a guided tour of the temple itself, a traditional breakfast, and a horse-drawn cart through the villages, followed by the smaller temples of Pawon and Mendut. On the third day choose between Merapi by jeep, through the old lava flows of Java’s most active volcano, or the Dieng Plateau, with its ancient temples and coloured crater lakes.

BorobudurPrambananPlataran Heritage
2Borneo

The River — Two Nights

Fly to Pangkalan Bun in Kalimantan and board a private klotok, a traditional wooden river boat with two cabins, air conditioning and a sundeck, for two nights on the rivers of Tanjung Puting National Park. Orangutans at the feeding stations at Tanjung Harapan, Camp Leakey and Pondok Tanggui, a guided walk in the forest with a ranger, and the riverbanks at dusk from the deck. All meals are on board.

Sekonyer RiverCamp LeakeyPrivate klotok
3Surabaya

The Port — One Night

Fly to Surabaya and stay at Hotel Majapahit, built in 1910 by a member of the Sarkies family, who built Raffles in Singapore. A morning walk through the old colonial quarter and the markets before the drive up to Bromo.

SurabayaHotel Majapahit
4Bromo

The Volcano — Two Nights

Plataran Bromo sits in the hills below the Tengger caldera. Before dawn, by private jeep to watch the sun rise over Bromo, Semeru and the sea of sand, then down onto the sand to the crater and the Poten temple, with a picnic breakfast on the savannah. In the afternoon, a cooking class with local ingredients.

Mount BromoPlataran Bromo
5Ubud

The Villages — Three Nights

A short flight to Bali and up to Tanadewa, in a courtyard villa with its own pool. A morning cycling through the rice fields and villages, with lunch with a local family. A day east to Penglipuran and its bamboo forest, the temple at Kehen, the beach at Virgin Beach, and the walled weaving village of Tenganan.

PenglipuranTengananTanadewa
6Lombok

The Sea — Three Nights

Across the strait to Tugu Lombok on Sire Beach, filled with antiques and old Indonesian art and facing the Gili islands. One morning snorkelling; the rest is rest, which by now you will have earned.

Gili IslandsTugu Lombok

Dates that matter

Borobudur is closed on Mondays. Tanjung Puting National Park closes from 15 to 31 January and from 1 to 15 June each year, and Bromo closes for a ceremony around Nyepi, the Balinese new year, which falls on 8 March in 2027. We build your dates around all three.

Every journey we publish is private, and this one can be shortened or softened: Borneo can be dropped, the boat can be swapped for a lodge, or the beach nights lengthened.
