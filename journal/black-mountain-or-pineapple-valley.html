Black Mountain or Pineapple Valley?

Hua Hin · Golf · 5 min read

Thailand · Hua Hin

Hua Hin is where Thailand’s serious golf lives, and a week here usually comes down to two courses. Black Mountain is the one with the reputation. Pineapple Valley is the one that surprises people. If there is time for only one of them, which should it be? And where do you sleep in a royal beach town that still keeps its evenings gentle?

— The short answer —

One round only? Black Mountain, for the course and the setting together.

Room for two? Play Pineapple Valley too. It is a close second, not a consolation.

Where to stay? Cape Nidhra, unless you want a resort, in which case Anantara Hua Hin.

Black Mountain: the reason people come

Black Mountain is why golfers come to Hua Hin. It hosts the Asian PGA Tour, it is kept to tour standard, and it gives you room off the tee before asking proper questions on the approach. The caddies are some of the best in Thailand. We route every Hua Hin week so this is the last round, played when you have found your rhythm, and we book the early tee time for the cooler air as much as the light.

Our founder’s verdict

If I could only play one, Black Mountain is where you’d find me in my golf clothes. Pineapple Valley is a close second, but the scenery as well as the course makes Black Mountain stand out.

Pineapple Valley: the one that surprises

The name is literal: the course is laid out over old pineapple plantation land. It rolls far more than you expect, and the variety holds your attention right through the back nine. It was named Asia’s Best New Course in 2009 and it has aged well. Most golfers arrive thinking of it as the warm-up for Black Mountain and leave talking about both.

With a week, there is no need to choose. Our Hua Hin golf week opens at Majestic Creek, plays Pineapple Valley in the middle and builds to Black Mountain.

Where to base the week

Cape Nidhra is a design-led, all-suite boutique hotel on the seafront, close to the town. It is the hotel we choose ourselves, and because of a long relationship with it, the week there costs no more than at the tier below.

Our founder’s verdict

Cape Nidhra is my go-to. I love smaller hotels with great service, and this is just what you get. It has a great location on the sands and near the town. If you like resorts, you won’t like it here. But if not, well, you may find your new Hua Hin home. We have.

If a resort is what you want, Anantara Hua Hin sits in mature gardens on the beach and is the name most people know here. For the quieter end of town, The Rock Hua Hin at Khao Takiab is our classic choice.

Hua Hin’s golf is at its best from November to March, and there is still time to play it this winter. Tell us your dates and who is travelling, and we will put the week together around them.
