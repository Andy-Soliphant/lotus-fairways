Hoiana Shores or Ba Na Hills?

Da Nang · Golf · 5 min read

Vietnam · Da Nang · Hoi An

Anyone planning a golf week on the Da Nang coast ends up asking the same two questions. If there is time for only one great round, which course? And where do you sleep, when the choice runs from a Four Seasons on the beach to a lively hotel a short walk from the bars?

We have played both courses and stayed in all three hotels below. Here is how we would choose.

— The short answer —

One round only? Hoiana Shores. It is our own choice, almost every time.

Prefer the mountains to the sea? Ba Na Hills, cooler and greener, and no step down.

Where to stay? Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai or Namia River Retreat for peace; Chicland if the evenings matter as much as the golf.

Hoiana Shores: the links

Robert Trent Jones II was given a stretch of natural dunes and left them to do most of the work. The result is real links golf, which is rarer in Asia than most golfers expect: the wind matters, the ground is firm, the ball runs, and the sea is in the corner of your eye for much of the round. The finishing holes are as good as anything in the region, and the conditioning matches the design.

It is also the round we put at the end of a Da Nang week, because it is the one guests talk about on the flight home.

Our founder’s verdict

I would be at Hoiana almost every time. I just love the course. It appeals to my eye, even if not always my game.

Ba Na Hills: the mountain course

Luke Donald’s course sits in the rolling foothills above Da Nang, and it is a completely different day out. The air is cooler, the grass greener, and the design uses the slopes well, with elevation changes that make several holes genuinely memorable. For a golfer who finds the coastal wind hard work, or who simply wants variety across a week, it is the perfect counterweight to Hoiana.

Our founder’s verdict

If I had to play Ba Na Hills, you wouldn’t hear a complaint from me. This is a win-win. You cannot lose when you are picking from the top.

With a week to play with, there is no need to choose. Our Da Nang & Hoi An golf week plays both, alongside Legend Da Nang, and finishes on Hoiana.

Where to base the week

This choice matters more than the course, because it decides what every evening looks like. The coast between Da Nang and Hoi An gives you two quite different weeks.

For peace and quiet: Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai sits on Ha My Beach between Hoi An and Da Nang, with villas on three tiers above the sea. It has long been the benchmark of Vietnamese luxury and still is. Namia River Retreat is the calmer, more intimate alternative, with spa treatments included, which is the perfect tonic after a round.

For the evenings: Chicland is in Da Nang itself, with beach views, easy access to the city’s bars and restaurants, and service that does everything you need very well. It is the better base for a group of friends who want the nineteenth hole to carry on into the night.

Our founder’s verdict

I prefer more peaceful surroundings, and depending on who I am with I would choose The Nam Hai or Namia River Retreat. If I were with friends and looking for more nightlife, I would probably pick Chicland or similar: more access to the bars and restaurants, but still near the beach, and with great service.
