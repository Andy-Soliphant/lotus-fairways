Rachamankha or Raya Heritage? Choosing in Chiang Mai

Thailand · Chiang Mai · 4 min read

Thailand · Chiang Mai

These two come from the same people, which tells you the standard before you arrive. They share a feeling for northern Thai craft and a refusal to shout. What separates them is one question: do you want to be inside Chiang Mai, or just outside it?

There is no wrong answer here, only the right one for you.

— The short answer —

Want to walk out of the door into the old city and its temples? Rachamankha.

Want the Ping river, quiet, and room to breathe? Raya Heritage.

Rachamankha: the old city at your door

Rachamankha sits inside the old city walls, right beside Wat Phra Singh, so the temples, the lanes and the morning markets start at the gate. The design is simple and assured, and the place has a stillness that is rare in the middle of a city.

It is our founder’s favourite in Chiang Mai, and has been since it opened.

Our founder’s verdict

My favourite in Chiang Mai, and has been since it opened. A great location, lovely simplistic design, and a feeling of coming home. And perhaps my favourite eggs Benedict anywhere.

Raya Heritage: the river, and room to breathe

Raya Heritage sits on the Ping river north of town, and it was built as a piece of advocacy for northern Thai craft: the textiles, ceramics and timber in the rooms were commissioned rather than bought in. It is quiet to the point of austere, in the best sense.

It suits travellers who have seen the old city, or who would rather visit it and come back to the river.

Our founder’s verdict

I pick Rachamankha for its premium location in the city, and Raya for those who want to be away from it. It isn’t right or wrong — it’s which is right for you.
