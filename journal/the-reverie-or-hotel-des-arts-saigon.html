The Reverie or Hotel des Arts? Where to Stay in Saigon

Vietnam · Saigon · 4 min read

Vietnam · Saigon

Saigon has no shortage of good hotels in District 1, and the difference between most of them is noise and view. These two are different. One is opulent on a scale few hotels anywhere attempt; the other is a love letter to 1930s Saigon with a rooftop pool on top.

Both are worth staying in. One is our first choice.

— The short answer —

Want the view, the service and the full sense of occasion? The Reverie. It is where we would stay.

Want design, period art and a rooftop pool above the city? Hotel des Arts. A little quieter, and full of character.

The Reverie: go through the doors

On paper The Reverie can look like too much. Italian furniture, bold colour, every surface decided. In person it works, and the reason is the service and the views: Saigon laid out around you from the upper floors of the Times Square building on Nguyen Hue.

It is the hotel for anyone who wants the city to feel like an occasion.

Our founder’s verdict

Online it can look a mismatch of styles. Walk through their doors and you are blown away with it all: modern, but not pretentious in any way, with captivating views of Saigon all around.

Hotel des Arts: 1930s Saigon, with a rooftop

Hotel des Arts is an Indochine fantasy that escapes pastiche because it commits: 1930s Saigon floor by floor, with real period art in the public rooms rather than prints. The rooftop is the real argument, a pool and a bar looking straight over District 1 as the lights come on.

It sits a step below The Reverie for us, but for travellers who care more about design than grandeur it is the more characterful stay.

Our founder’s verdict

Des Arts sits just below The Reverie. Given the choice I’d spend my time at The Reverie — brilliant location, service on point, and stunning views.

Saigon rewards two or three nights rather than one, especially at the start or end of a Vietnam journey. See the full picture in where to stay in Saigon, and if you are playing while you are there, golf in Saigon.
