Trisara or Amanpuri?

Thailand · Phuket · 5 min read

Thailand · Phuket

These are the two names that come up whenever someone asks for the best hotel in Phuket, and both deserve to. They sit on the same west coast, both are built as villas and pavilions rather than blocks of rooms, and both exist to make you forget there is a busy island behind you.

We have stayed at both. They are special in different ways, and we do have a favourite.

— The short answer —

Want a private bay, a villa pool and a Michelin star on site? Trisara. It is our own choice.

Want the original Aman, and all the space that comes with it? Amanpuri. Nothing else in Asia carries quite that history.

Trisara: the private bay

Trisara sits on a private headland above Nai Thon, on Phuket’s quieter north-west coast. The villas are stepped down through the trees so that each has its own pool and its own piece of the view, and there are no day guests and no through-traffic. The beach below is, in practice, the hotel’s own.

Then there is the food. PRU holds a Michelin star and sources heavily from Trisara’s own farm, which means one of the best meals on the island is a short walk from your villa.

Our founder’s verdict

Amazing views, amazing service, stunning villas. When can I go back?

Amanpuri: the original

Amanpuri opened in 1988 and began a brand that changed luxury travel. Ed Tuttle’s pavilions sit in a coconut grove above Pansea Beach, laid out so you see almost nobody. The black-tiled pool is on every list, and what does not photograph is how quiet it has stayed over nearly forty years while its imitators got louder.

The estate is large, and that is the trade-off. Distances are real, and the buggies are part of daily life. For some guests that space is exactly the point.

Our founder’s verdict

Both are special, but the size of Amanpuri and needing a buggy everywhere just isn’t for me. The villas, though, are amazing. For my own money, Trisara wins.
