The Trip

Three championship courses, a beach base of your choosing, and a week paced the way you'd actually want to play it.

Phuket is where most international golfers begin their Thailand journey — and rarely their last visit. Laguna, Red Mountain and Blue Canyon give you three of Asia's most respected layouts inside a week, each within an easy transfer of the west-coast beaches.

This is a seven-night trip built around three rounds, leaving genuine room to breathe — a day on the sand, a long lunch, a sunset somewhere quiet. Green fees, caddies and private transfers are all handled. You choose the hotel; everything else is arranged around it.

Built on thirty years of knowing these courses and the people who run them — not a booking engine's guess.

The Week

A suggested shape — every day flexes to your group, your handicaps and your pace. Nothing here is fixed in stone.

Day1

Arrival

Private transfer from Phuket International to your hotel. Evening at leisure to settle in — dinner recommendations arranged to taste.

Day2

Laguna Golf Phuket Round 1

An accessible, beautifully conditioned resort course to open the week — flowing fairways set among lagoons, a gentle reintroduction to Phuket golf.

Day3

At leisure

Beach, spa or exploring — Surin and Kata for the quieter side of the island, Patong if the group wants the noise.

Day4

Red Mountain Golf Club Round 2

Carved from a former tin mine, Red Mountain is Phuket's most dramatic round — bold elevation changes and one of the island's signature golfing days.

Day5

At leisure

A second free day to spend as you like — a boat to the islands, a long lunch, or simply nothing at all.

Day6

Blue Canyon — Canyon Course Round 3

The island's championship benchmark, host to tour events and a genuine test off the back tees. A fitting finish to the golfing week.

Day7

At leisure

A final full day on the coast — beach, spa, or a farewell dinner somewhere memorable.

Day8

Departure

Private transfer to Phuket International in good time for your flight home.
