Most Thailand honeymoons are a city and a beach. This one gives you the country in between: five nights in the old walled city of Chiang Mai, two on a lake in the jungle of Khao Sok, and a beach on the Gulf so quiet that the dolphins are pink and the tourists are few.

It ends where honeymoons should, with four nights and nothing planned.

The Journey

1Bangkok

After Dark — Three Nights

Three nights at Hotel Muse, with its rooftop pool and speakeasy bar. The first evening by tuk-tuk once the heat has gone: Chinatown’s food stalls, the flower market at Pak Khlong Talat, Wat Pho lit up and nearly empty, and a drink above the city. A day by river and longtail to the Grand Palace, the Emerald Buddha, Wat Pho and the canals of Thonburi. And a last night among the hidden bars behind unmarked doors, where the drinks are made to your taste.

Chao PhrayaHotel Muse Bangkok
2Chiang Mai

The Old City — Five Nights

North to Rachamankha, inside the old city walls beside Wat Phra Singh. A day of white water on the Mae Taeng river and off-road through the valley. A day with a Chiang Mai family in a house that has been theirs for a century and a half, choosing ingredients from the garden and cooking northern dishes you will not find on a restaurant menu. A day in the forest in the company of elephants. And two days to walk the temples of the old city.

Mae TaengRachamankha
3Khao Sok

The Lake — Two Nights

A flight south and a longtail across Chiew Larn lake, between limestone towers, to 500 Rai Floating Resort: villas on the water in Khao Sok national park, with kayaks at your door. Sunrise on the lake looking for hornbills, and nothing else asked of you.

Chiew Larn Lake500 Rai Floating Resort
4Khanom

The Quiet Coast — Three Nights

To Aava Resort on one of the last unspoilt stretches of the Gulf coast. Out by longtail with a local fisherman to find the pink dolphins that live off this shore, one of the few places they can still be seen, and to the island of Koh Nui Nork. A morning paddling the rapids of Khlong Klai through the forest.

Gulf of ThailandAava Resort
5Koh Samui

The Beach — Four Nights

By ferry to Koh Samui and a garden pool room at Buri Rasa Village, a small hotel built like a Thai village, set back from the road on the beach at Chaweng, with breakfast served all day. Four nights with nothing in them.

Buri Rasa Village
