There has always been a railway down the length of Vietnam. Until now there has not been a train you would choose for its own sake. SJourney changes that: a private suite, a dining car with its own chefs, and seven nights covering the whole country, with guided stops along the way.

It is the most relaxed way we know to see Vietnam from end to end. You unpack once, and the country comes to you.

The Journey

1Hanoi

Departure

The journey begins in the capital. Most guests arrive a night or two early to see the city first; we suggest the Metropole or Capella Hanoi.

2Ninh Binh

The Karsts

The limestone towers and river valleys south of Hanoi, often called Ha Long Bay on land.

3Quang Binh

The Caves

The province of Phong Nha-Ke Bang, a national park of forest and some of the largest caves on earth.

4Hue

The Imperial City

The seat of the Nguyen emperors, with its citadel and royal tombs along the Perfume River.

Perfume River
5Hoi An

The Old Town

The merchant town on the Thu Bon, its lanes of shophouses and assembly halls little changed in two centuries.

Thu Bon
6Phu Yen

The Quiet Coast

One of the least visited stretches of Vietnam’s coastline, all headlands and empty bays.

7Binh Thuan

The South

The dunes and fishing harbours of the far south coast, before the last run into the city.

8Ho Chi Minh City

Arrival

The journey ends in Saigon. Stay on for a night or two at The Reverie or Park Hyatt Saigon, or fly straight on to the beach.
